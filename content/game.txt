# Rust-Based PC Game
> A survival PC game set in a post-apocalyptic world, many years after the war between machines and magic.
> Work in progress: these are my early design notes and they will change as development continues.

## Character Creation

### Character
- Category: Player / NPC
- Graphic assets: 3D
- Creation flow: Loading screen → Character creation
- Character slots: 4
- Characters cannot be edited once creation is done

### Attributes
- Health: 100 points
- Hunger: 100
- Thirst: 100
- Oxygen: 100
- Temperature: 25° – 45°
- Agility: variable (depends on food)
- Strength: variable (50)
- Intel: variable (50)
- Stamina: pool size = Strength
- Mana: pool size = Intel

### What Attributes Do
- Strength: damage, health regen, stamina pool size
- Agility: stamina regen boost, sprint speed, hold time (climbing)
- Intel: mana pool size

### Appearance
- Body type: male / female (female adds body shape and long hair options)
- Customise: hair colour, eye colour, face shape, skin colour
- Height: max 1 unit, min ½ unit

## World Creation

### Setting
- World: post-apocalyptic, many years after the Machine vs Magic war
- Seasons: summer, winter
- Weather: windy, storms, heavy rain, acid rain, poison wind waves, heat waves

### World Limits
- Character maximum height: 1 unit
- Top edge (maximum height): +1000, death ray
- Bottom edge (maximum depth): −1000, red lava

### Procedural Generation Map
- Mountain peak: 750
- Maximum structure height: 800
- Open caves: +600 to −400
- Deepest ocean: −500
- Deepest cave: −900

### Biome: Desert
- Materials: sand, quicksand, hard sand, stone, sulfur, lava, cactus, short bush
- Weather events: sandstorms
- Structures: pyramids, ruins, tombs
- Temperature: 40° – 55°
- Biome depth: −100
- Water pods: dry grass, palm and cactus trees

### Biome: Wastelands
- Materials: concrete powder, sulfur, lava pockets, dry soil, poisoned water, poisoned air, scrap yards, bones, crystal shards, mud pits
- Temperature: 35° – 55°
- Biome depth: 150
