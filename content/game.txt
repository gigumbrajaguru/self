# Lini: Huma
> A 3D survival game for PC · In development
Long after machines and magic went to war, the world still burns. Now it's your turn to survive what they left behind.

## The World After the War
Many years ago, machines and magic fought a war that nobody won. Cities fell, the skies turned hostile and the land itself was poisoned. Lini: Huma begins long after the last battle, in the broken world the war left behind.
The world still turns through its seasons, but the weather is no longer just weather. Every journey depends on reading the sky.
### ☀️ | Summer and Winter
Two seasons that change how far you can travel, what you need to carry and how long you can last out there.
### 🌪️ | Wind and Storms
Strong winds, storms and heavy rain roll across the land, hiding the path ahead and slowing every step.
### ☣️ | Acid Rain and Poison Winds
Some rain burns. Some winds carry poison in waves. When they come, you'd better already know where to hide.
### 🔥 | Heat Waves
Days so hot that water runs out faster than you can find it. Survivors learn to travel when the heat breaks.

## Becoming a Survivor
Every journey starts with a survivor. You have four character slots, and every survivor you create is yours for good. Once creation is done there's no going back, so every choice matters.
Choose male or female, then shape who you are: hair colour, eye colour, face shape, skin colour, body shape and long hair. Some survivors stand tall, others are half their size.
Staying alive means watching your body as closely as the horizon.
### ❤️ | Health
Your life. Lose it all and the wasteland wins.
### 🍖 | Hunger
Food keeps you moving, and what you eat shapes how agile you are.
### 💧 | Thirst
Clean water is rare in a poisoned world. Every drop counts.
### 🫁 | Oxygen
Deep water, buried caves and poisoned air will test how long you can hold on.
### 🌡️ | Body Temperature
Too hot or too cold and your body starts to fail. The seasons and weather never stop pushing.

## Three Paths to Power
How you survive depends on how you grow. Three attributes shape every survivor: Strength powers your stamina, Intel powers your mana, and Agility is fed by the food you find.
### 💪 | Strength
Hit harder, recover health faster and carry a deeper pool of stamina for the long fights.
### 🏃 | Agility
Sprint faster, hold on longer while climbing and recover stamina sooner. Eat well and you'll move like the wind.
### 🔮 | Intel
Magic didn't die in the war. The sharper your mind, the deeper your pool of mana.

## From the Death Ray to the Red Lava | layers
The world of Lini: Huma is procedurally generated, so no two worlds are the same, and it reaches far in both directions. At the very top, a death ray seals the sky. Far below, there's nothing but red lava. Everything in between is yours to explore.
### ☄️ | The Death Ray
The roof of the world. Climb too high and nothing survives.
### 🏙️ | Towering Ruins
What's left of the old world. Some ruins rise higher than the mountains themselves.
### 🏔️ | Mountain Peaks
The highest natural ground, with a view across everything the war destroyed.
### 🕳️ | Open Caves
Caves that open to the sky and run from the highlands deep beneath the surface.
### 🌊 | The Deep Ocean
Dark water that sinks far below the land. Watch your oxygen.
### ⛏️ | The Deepest Caves
Tunnels that reach almost to the bottom of the world, for those brave enough to follow them.
### 🌋 | The Red Lava
The floor of the world. Nothing comes back from below.

## Lands You'll Cross
The surface is split into biomes, each with its own ground, weather and dangers. The first two are already shaped, and they're the lands the war hit hardest.
### 🏜️ | The Desert
Scorching heat over sand, quicksand and hard sand, broken by stone, sulfur and pockets of lava. Cactus and short bushes are the only cover, and sandstorms can swallow the way home. Hidden among the dunes wait pyramids, ruins and tombs, and rare water pods where dry grass, palms and cactus trees still grow.
### ☢️ | The Wastelands
What the war left behind: concrete powder, scrap yards, bones, mud pits and dry, cracked soil. The water is poisoned, the air is poisoned and lava pockets wait beneath your feet. It's almost as hot as the desert, but somewhere in the rubble, crystal shards are waiting to be found.

## Every Corner of the World | regions
The Desert and the Wastelands are only the beginning. The war touched every land differently: some froze, some drowned, some burned and some were torn right out of the ground. Every one of them is waiting to be explored.
### ❄️ | The Frozen North
Where frost magic broke loose and never stopped. The cold drains body heat fast, so fire and shelter decide who makes it through the night.
- Snowfields — Flat white silence, broken only by the frozen wrecks of war machines.
- Ice Spires — Towers of ice that burst from the ground where frost spells struck.
- Snowbound Pines — Dark pine forests under heavy snow: shelter from the wind, but little food.
- Frozen Shore — Grey sand and ice where the sea meets the cold.
- Frost Grove — Quiet hillside groves buried in powder snow.
- Snow Slopes — Steep mountainsides where one wrong step starts a slide.
- Jagged Peaks — Knife-edged summits that drain a climber's stamina.
- Glacier Crowns — Mountaintops locked under packed ice that never melts.
- Frozen Rivers — The fastest roads through the north, as long as the ice holds.
- Frozen Sea — Icebergs drifting over black water.
- Deep Frozen Sea — Freezing depths beneath the ice, where oxygen runs out fast.
### 🌲 | The Cold Wilds
Windswept highlands and old forests that the war mostly passed by. Wood and game are plentiful, but so are the things that hunt them.
- Pine Taiga — Cool spruce forests full of game and timber.
- Ancient Pines — Giant pines that were already old before the war began.
- Elder Spruce Woods — Towering spruce where light barely reaches the ground.
- Windswept Hills — Bare hills where the wind never rests.
- Gravel Hills — Loose stone slopes still scarred by old artillery.
- Wind-Bent Woods — Trees bent sideways by a hundred years of storms.
- Stone Shore — Rocky coasts battered by grey waves.
- Stone Peaks — Bare summits of cold grey rock.
- Cold Sea — Chilly waters rich with fish.
- Deep Cold Sea — Dark trenches where wrecked warships rest.
### 🌿 | The Green Remnants
Lands where life fought back. Food and clean water are easier to find here, which is exactly why survivors fight over them.
- Plains — Open grassland and the easiest place to start over.
- Sunflower Fields — Golden fields that somehow survived the fire.
- Forest — Mixed woods with timber, berries and cover.
- Wildflower Woods — Forest floors carpeted in flowers, with traces of old magic in the soil.
- Birch Woods — Pale, quiet birch forests.
- Tall Birch Woods — Ancient birches that stretch far above the canopy.
- Darkwood — A canopy so thick that midday feels like night, and things hunt in the shade.
- Ashen Grove — Grey, drained trees where the war pulled the magic out of the land.
- Highland Meadow — Calm mountain meadows full of flowers and bees.
- Cherry Blossom Hills — Pink-blossomed slopes, the most peaceful place left in the world.
- Fungal Isles — Remote islands of giant mushrooms, strangely untouched by the war's monsters.
- Swamp — Murky water and twisted trees, poisoned in places.
- Mangrove Marsh — Tangled roots over thick mud that slows every step.
- Overgrown Jungle — Vines and giant trees that swallowed whole cities.
- Jungle Edge — Where the jungle thins out into open ground.
- Bamboo Thickets — Dense bamboo forests that are hard to cross and easy to get lost in.
### 🏜️ | The Sunscorched South
The hottest lands, where the machine armies fought longest. Beyond the Desert and the Wastelands, the heat bakes everything it touches.
- Savanna — Dry grasslands under flat-topped trees.
- Savanna Plateau — Raised grasslands with views across the burning south.
- Shattered Savanna — Wild, broken ground where blasts pushed the earth into towers.
- Red Badlands — Layered red clay canyons with old mines dug deep inside.
- Wooded Mesa — Badlands crowned with stubborn, dry woodland.
- Eroded Spires — Clay spires carved thin by years of acid rain.
### 🌊 | The Drowned World
The seas rose after the war, swallowing coastlines and cities. Everything below the surface is a race against your oxygen.
- Beaches — Sandy coasts littered with what the tide brings back.
- Rivers — Lifelines of fresh water running through every land.
- Open Sea — Endless water with sunken ruins below.
- Deep Sea — Dark depths where the remains of naval machines lie.
- Mild Sea — Gentle, warm-ish waters full of life.
- Deep Mild Sea — Deeper waters with ruins waiting to be found.
- Coral Sea — Bright coral reefs growing over the wrecks of the war.
### 🕳️ | Beneath the Surface
The world goes down a long way. The deeper you go, the less air you have and the stranger things get.
- Dripstone Caverns — Caves where stalactites hang like spears.
- Glowroot Caverns — Lush underground gardens lit by glowing roots.
- The Silent Deep — The deepest caves, where something listens for every sound you make.
### 🌋 | The Molten Deep
Close to the red lava at the bottom of the world, heat and leftover magic twisted the land into something else entirely.
- Ember Wastes — Red rock, fire pools and air that burns to breathe.
- Valley of Lost Souls — Blue-fire valleys of grasping sand, scattered with the giant bones of the fallen.
- Crimson Woods — Forests of red fungal trees that pulse like something alive.
- Twisted Woods — Teal fungal forests warped by stray magic.
- Basalt Deltas — Ash-choked fields of black basalt pillars.
### ☄️ | The Shattered Sky
When the war ended, magic tore pieces of the world loose. They still drift high above the land, just beneath the death ray.
- The Last Isle — A pale island hanging in darkness, guarded by something ancient.
- Sky Highlands — The tallest of the drifting lands, with ruined towers of the old mages.
- Sky Midlands — Rolling stone fields floating in the void.
- Sky Barrens — Empty, broken edges of the floating world.
- Drifting Islets — Small fragments of land scattered across the sky.
