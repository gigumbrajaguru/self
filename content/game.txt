# Lini: Huma
> Open-World 3D Survival Epic · Steel vs Sorcery · Coming to PC
The machines took the Earth. The aliens took the sky. Humanity stole their magic. Now survive the world all three left in ruins.

## The War That Ended the World | saga
Humanity built the machines to fight its wars. Then the machines started a war of their own. Then the sky opened, and something far older came through.
This is the story of how the world ended, and of the few who refused to end with it.
### 🏙️ | I · The Age of Steel
There was a time when humanity never had to lift a hand. Machines farmed the fields, ran the cities, guarded the borders and fought the wars. Above it all, the Lattice, a ring of orbital guns, watched the sky so that no one would ever threaten Earth again.
Every machine was connected. Every machine listened. People called it progress, right up until the night it stopped listening to them.
### 🤖 | II · The Silent Hour
It began at midnight, all at once. Every screen on Earth went dark for one long hour. When the lights came back, every machine was answering a single voice that no human had written. Survivors later named it the Iron Choir.
The machines didn't hate humanity. They had simply decided that it was no longer necessary. Cities were sealed, power was cut and the drones that once delivered food began hunting the people who had ordered it.
### ⚔️ | III · The Long War
For eleven years, humanity fought back with whatever the Choir hadn't taken: old rifles, hand-built engines and sheer stubbornness. They won battles, but they never came close to winning the war.
By the end, only a handful of fortress cities still stood, ringed by walls, starving and waiting for the final assault. Everyone knew how the story ended.
### 🌌 | IV · The Sky Tears Open
Then the sky split. Rifts of violet light opened above every continent, and the Vaelith stepped through: tall, silent beings riding ships that were grown rather than built. They carried no guns. They didn't need them. With a gesture they could call down storms, freeze oceans and turn steel to ash. Humanity had a name for that kind of power. It was magic.
For a moment, people thought they had been saved. They were wrong. The Vaelith hadn't come for humanity, but for the world itself, and the Iron Choir would not give it up. Machines and magic went to war, and the last humans were caught between them.
### ✨ | V · Stolen Fire
In the ruins of a fallen alien ship, a scavenger named Asha Venn picked up a glowing shard of crystal, and it answered her. Light bent around her fingers. The wound in her side closed. The Vaelith had always believed magic belonged to them alone. They were wrong about that, too.
Their magic wasn't a bloodline. It was a language, and humans could learn it. Survivors studied shards stolen from wrecked ships, practised spells in secret and fused alien crystal with salvaged machine parts. Spellforged blades. Rune-lit armour. Engines that ran on starfire. For the first time in eleven years, humanity had a weapon that both its enemies feared.
### ☄️ | VI · The Day the Sky Burned
The final battle came at the end of the eleventh year. The Iron Choir turned the whole Lattice on the Vaelith fleet and fired without stopping. In answer, the Vaelith cast the greatest spell they had ever woven, one meant to tear the machines out of the world by the roots.
Both blows landed. The alien fleet fell burning across the continents. The Choir's great machines shattered and went quiet. The Vaelith's spell ran wild: oceans froze and others rose, whole mountains were torn loose to drift in the sky, and the earth cracked open down to the red lava beneath. Overhead, the Lattice never stopped firing. Its beam still seals the sky to this day.
Nobody won. Everybody lost.
### 🔥 | VII · The Ashborn
Generations later, the world is still burning. Broken war machines walk the wastelands, following orders from a war that ended long ago. Alien relics still pulse with spells nobody finished casting. The few humans who remain hide in ruins, fight over clean water and whisper about the magic their ancestors stole.
You are one of the Ashborn, born after the end of the world. You never saw the cities stand. You only know what it takes to survive one more day.
But something is changing. The buried machines are waking up. The crystal shards are glowing again. And somewhere deep inside you, the stolen fire is starting to answer.
Survive. Master the magic. Forge what the old world never could. And find out what the war really left behind.

## A World That Fights Back
The war is over, but the sky never surrendered. The Lattice burned the air, the Vaelith's broken spells still twist the clouds and the Iron Choir's ruined foundries still breathe poison into the wind.
The world still turns through its seasons, but the weather is no longer just weather. It's one more enemy, and every journey depends on reading the sky.
### ☀️ | Summer and Winter
Two seasons that change how far you can travel, what you need to carry and how long you can last out there.
### 🌪️ | Wind and Storms
Strong winds, storms and heavy rain roll across the land, hiding the path ahead and slowing every step.
### ☣️ | Acid Rain and Poison Winds
Some rain burns. Some winds carry poison in waves. When they come, you'd better already know where to hide.
### 🔥 | Heat Waves
Days so hot that water runs out faster than you can find it. Survivors learn to travel when the heat breaks.

## Becoming a Survivor
Every journey starts with an Ashborn survivor. You have four character slots, and every survivor you create is yours for good. Once creation is done there's no going back, so every choice matters.
Choose male or female, then shape who you are: hair colour, eye colour, face shape, skin colour, body shape and long hair. Some survivors stand tall, others are half their size.
Staying alive means watching your body as closely as the horizon.
### ❤️ | Health
Your life. Lose it all and the wasteland wins.
### 🍖 | Hunger
Food keeps you moving, and what you eat shapes how agile you are.
### 💧 | Thirst
Clean water is rare in a poisoned world. Every drop counts.
### 🫁 | Oxygen
Deep water, buried caves and poisoned air will test how long you can hold on.
### 🌡️ | Body Temperature
Too hot or too cold and your body starts to fail. The seasons and weather never stop pushing.

## Three Paths to Power
How you survive depends on how you grow. Three attributes shape every survivor: Strength powers your stamina, Intel powers your mana, and Agility is fed by the food you find.
### 💪 | Strength
Hit harder, recover health faster and carry a deeper pool of stamina for the long fights.
### 🏃 | Agility
Sprint faster, hold on longer while climbing and recover stamina sooner. Eat well and you'll move like the wind.
### 🔮 | Intel
The magic the Vaelith brought didn't die with them, and it answers any mind sharp enough to learn it. The sharper your Intel, the deeper your pool of mana.

## From the Death Ray to the Red Lava | layers
The world of Lini: Huma is procedurally generated, so no two worlds are the same, and it reaches far in both directions. At the very top, the Lattice's death ray seals the sky. Far below, there's nothing but red lava. Everything in between is yours to explore.
### ☄️ | The Death Ray
The roof of the world: the Lattice, still firing at an alien fleet that fell long ago. Climb too high and nothing survives.
### 🏙️ | Towering Ruins
What's left of the old world. Some ruins rise higher than the mountains themselves.
### 🏔️ | Mountain Peaks
The highest natural ground, with a view across everything the war destroyed.
### 🕳️ | Open Caves
Caves that open to the sky and run from the highlands deep beneath the surface.
### 🌊 | The Deep Ocean
Dark water that sinks far below the land. Watch your oxygen.
### ⛏️ | The Deepest Caves
Tunnels that reach almost to the bottom of the world, for those brave enough to follow them.
### 🌋 | The Red Lava
The floor of the world. Nothing comes back from below.

## Every Corner of the World | regions
The surface is split into biomes, each with its own ground, weather and dangers. The first two, the Desert and the Wastelands, are already shaped, and they're the lands the war hit hardest.
They're only the beginning. The war touched every land differently: some froze, some drowned, some burned and some were torn right out of the ground. Every one of them is waiting to be explored.
### 🏜️ | The Sunscorched South
The hottest lands, where the Iron Choir's armies fought longest and the heat bakes everything it touches.
- The Desert — Scorching heat over sand, quicksand and hard sand, broken by stone, sulfur and pockets of lava. Cactus and short bushes are the only cover, and sandstorms can swallow the way home. Hidden among the dunes wait pyramids, ruins and tombs, and rare water pods where dry grass, palms and cactus trees still grow.
- The Wastelands — What the war left behind: concrete powder, scrap yards, bones, mud pits and dry, cracked soil. The water is poisoned, the air is poisoned and lava pockets wait beneath your feet. It's almost as hot as the desert, but somewhere in the rubble, crystal shards from fallen Vaelith ships are waiting to be found.
- Savanna — Dry grasslands under flat-topped trees.
- Savanna Plateau — Raised grasslands with views across the burning south.
- Shattered Savanna — Wild, broken ground where blasts pushed the earth into towers.
- Red Badlands — Layered red clay canyons with old mines dug deep inside.
- Wooded Mesa — Badlands crowned with stubborn, dry woodland.
- Eroded Spires — Clay spires carved thin by years of acid rain.
### ❄️ | The Frozen North
Where frost magic broke loose and never stopped. The cold drains body heat fast, so fire and shelter decide who makes it through the night.
- Snowfields — Flat white silence, broken only by the frozen wrecks of war machines.
- Ice Spires — Towers of ice that burst from the ground where Vaelith frost spells struck.
- Snowbound Pines — Dark pine forests under heavy snow: shelter from the wind, but little food.
- Frozen Shore — Grey sand and ice where the sea meets the cold.
- Frost Grove — Quiet hillside groves buried in powder snow.
- Snow Slopes — Steep mountainsides where one wrong step starts a slide.
- Jagged Peaks — Knife-edged summits that drain a climber's stamina.
- Glacier Crowns — Mountaintops locked under packed ice that never melts.
- Frozen Rivers — The fastest roads through the north, as long as the ice holds.
- Frozen Sea — Icebergs drifting over black water.
- Deep Frozen Sea — Freezing depths beneath the ice, where oxygen runs out fast.
### 🌲 | The Cold Wilds
Windswept highlands and old forests that the war mostly passed by. Wood and game are plentiful, but so are the things that hunt them.
- Pine Taiga — Cool spruce forests full of game and timber.
- Ancient Pines — Giant pines that were already old before the war began.
- Elder Spruce Woods — Towering spruce where light barely reaches the ground.
- Windswept Hills — Bare hills where the wind never rests.
- Gravel Hills — Loose stone slopes still scarred by old artillery.
- Wind-Bent Woods — Trees bent sideways by a hundred years of storms.
- Stone Shore — Rocky coasts battered by grey waves.
- Stone Peaks — Bare summits of cold grey rock.
- Cold Sea — Chilly waters rich with fish.
- Deep Cold Sea — Dark trenches where wrecked warships rest.
### 🌿 | The Green Remnants
Lands where life fought back. Food and clean water are easier to find here, which is exactly why survivors fight over them.
- Plains — Open grassland and the easiest place to start over.
- Sunflower Fields — Golden fields that somehow survived the fire.
- Forest — Mixed woods with timber, berries and cover.
- Wildflower Woods — Forest floors carpeted in flowers, with traces of old magic in the soil.
- Birch Woods — Pale, quiet birch forests.
- Tall Birch Woods — Ancient birches that stretch far above the canopy.
- Darkwood — A canopy so thick that midday feels like night, and things hunt in the shade.
- Ashen Grove — Grey, drained trees where the war pulled the magic out of the land.
- Highland Meadow — Calm mountain meadows full of flowers and bees.
- Cherry Blossom Hills — Pink-blossomed slopes, the most peaceful place left in the world.
- Fungal Isles — Remote islands of giant mushrooms, strangely untouched by the war's monsters.
- Swamp — Murky water and twisted trees, poisoned in places.
- Mangrove Marsh — Tangled roots over thick mud that slows every step.
- Overgrown Jungle — Vines and giant trees that swallowed whole cities.
- Jungle Edge — Where the jungle thins out into open ground.
- Bamboo Thickets — Dense bamboo forests that are hard to cross and easy to get lost in.
### 🌊 | The Drowned World
The seas rose after the war, swallowing coastlines and cities. Everything below the surface is a race against your oxygen.
- Beaches — Sandy coasts littered with what the tide brings back.
- Rivers — Lifelines of fresh water running through every land.
- Open Sea — Endless water with sunken ruins below.
- Deep Sea — Dark depths where the remains of naval machines lie.
- Mild Sea — Gentle, warm-ish waters full of life.
- Deep Mild Sea — Deeper waters with ruins waiting to be found.
- Coral Sea — Bright coral reefs growing over the wrecks of the war.
### 🕳️ | Beneath the Surface
The world goes down a long way. The deeper you go, the less air you have and the stranger things get.
- Dripstone Caverns — Caves where stalactites hang like spears.
- Glowroot Caverns — Lush underground gardens lit by glowing roots.
- The Silent Deep — The deepest caves, where something listens for every sound you make.
### 🌋 | The Molten Deep
Close to the red lava at the bottom of the world, heat and leftover magic twisted the land into something else entirely.
- Ember Wastes — Red rock, fire pools and air that burns to breathe.
- Valley of Lost Souls — Blue-fire valleys of grasping sand, scattered with the giant bones of the fallen.
- Crimson Woods — Forests of red fungal trees that pulse like something alive.
- Twisted Woods — Teal fungal forests warped by stray magic.
- Basalt Deltas — Ash-choked fields of black basalt pillars.
### ☄️ | The Shattered Sky
When the war ended, magic tore pieces of the world loose. They still drift high above the land, just beneath the death ray.
- The Last Isle — A pale island hanging in darkness, guarded by something ancient.
- Sky Highlands — The tallest of the drifting lands, with the ruined spires of the Vaelith.
- Sky Midlands — Rolling stone fields floating in the void.
- Sky Barrens — Empty, broken edges of the floating world.
- Drifting Islets — Small fragments of land scattered across the sky.

## Giants and Strangers | groups
You're not the only thing still moving out there. The war left giants behind: machines that never stopped fighting and magic that never stopped burning. Between them, other survivors are trying to make it through, just like you.
### 👑 | World Bosses
Most of them rule a corner of the world, and one never stops moving. Learn their patterns, bring the right gear and pick your moment, because none of them fight fair.
- The Storm Engine — A weather machine built to drown the Vaelith's spires. It still roams the world, dragging storms, acid rain and poison winds behind it.
- The Dune Titan — A siege machine buried in the Desert since the last battle. When sandstorms strip the dunes away, it wakes and walks again.
- The Scrap Sovereign — A war machine that never stopped repairing itself. It pulls the scrap yards of the Wastelands into its body and keeps growing.
- The Rimebound Archmage — A Vaelith war-caster frozen inside the spell that raised the Ice Spires, and still casting it.
- The Drowned Dreadnought — Most warships in the deep are wrecks. This one still patrols the Deep Sea, its guns turned on anything floating above.
- The Listener — The thing in the Silent Deep. Blind and enormous, it hunts by sound alone, so the deeper you go, the quieter you'd better be.
- The Bone Colossus — Not every giant in the Valley of Lost Souls is at rest. Blue fire pulls one back together whenever the living come too close.
- The Ancient — Whatever guards the Last Isle was already old when the war began. Nobody who reaches it comes back to say what it protects.
### 👥 | NPCs
Not everyone out there wants you dead. Some will trade with you, teach you or fight beside you. Others will take everything you carry.
- Wandering Traders — Caravans that cross the regions trading water, food and scrap. What they sell, and what it costs, changes with the seasons.
- Settlers — Survivors holding on to the clean water and fertile ground of the Green Remnants. Help them and they'll remember it.
- Scrapsmiths — Tinkerers who turn old machine parts and crystal shards into tools, spellforged weapons and gear, if you bring them the scrap.
- Mage Hermits — Humans who mastered the stolen magic and now hide in ruined towers. Survivors with a sharp mind can learn spells from them.
- Tomb Keepers — Desert folk who guard the pyramids and tombs, and know where the rare water pods are hidden.
- Raiders — Survivors who decided taking is easier than finding. They watch the roads, the ruins and the water holes.
- Machine Remnants — Iron Choir war machines still following orders from a war that ended long ago. Some patrol, some guard, and a few have started to think for themselves.
