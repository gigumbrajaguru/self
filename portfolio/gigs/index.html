FAQ

Quick answers before we talk

How much does a bot cost?

Every bot starts at $100. The final price depends on the features, platforms and integrations you need, and is confirmed after I analyse your requirements.

How do we get started?

Book a 15-minute call on Calendly — available slots are shown in your own timezone (I'm based in Sri Lanka, UTC+5:30). We'll go through your needs and I'll send you a quote and plan.

Are third-party costs included?

Platform and service fees — such as hosting, WhatsApp Business or X API usage, and AI model usage — are billed by those providers and discussed upfront in your quote.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. You'll get a timeline together with your quote, plus regular updates while your bot is being built.

Can you maintain or extend my bot later?

Yes. Post-launch fixes, new features and ongoing maintenance can be included in your quote or arranged later.
